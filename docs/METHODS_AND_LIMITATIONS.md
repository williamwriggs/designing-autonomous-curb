# Methods and Limitations

The archive is intended to support transparent review of the spatial and temporal analyses reported in the paper. The event-level file contains 4,080 synthetic passenger pick-up and drop-off observations created for exploratory analysis of concentration, timing, behavioral sorting, and curb-design implications.

## Interpretation

- The records are synthetic and privacy-preserving.
- Generalized polygons are analytical units, not recommended permanent operating zones.
- The study is geographically bounded and should not be generalized to citywide autonomous-vehicle behavior.
- The dataset does not include observed dwell time.
- Pick-up and drop-off shares should not be treated as population estimates.
- The archive supports reproducibility of descriptive spatial and temporal analysis, not validation of proprietary fleet operations.

## Reproducibility roadmap

The final public archive should contain:

1. the synthetic event CSV and data dictionary;
2. the final manuscript PDF;
3. publication figures in SVG or PDF form;
4. analysis scripts used to generate each figure;
5. a tagged release corresponding to the submitted manuscript; and
6. a permanent DOI through Zenodo or another research repository.
